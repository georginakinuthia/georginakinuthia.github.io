Circular Care Infrastructure

Nairobi & Kisumu, Kenya / 2025

The Civic Insect Farm Facilities Blueprint

Developed as a core component of the FAO Green Cities Initiative in partnership with AICS. This bioconversion prototype optimizes a 50x100 ft plot footprint to convert 5 tons of daily organic waste into high-protein animal feed and nutrient-rich compost using low-carbon compressed earth blocks (CEB) and solar arrays. Designed around micro-franchise scaling strategies to anchor local youth cooperatives.

Kitui, Kenya / 2021

Feminist Republik Healing Farm

A women-led sanctuary masterplan designed with soft curves and clustered layouts following natural contours to support African Women Human Rights Defenders (AWHRDs). Deployed an array of passive solar chimney ventilation steps, high thermal-mass adobe barriers, rainwater storage networks, and universally accessible paraplegic modules built out of stone, bamboo, and compressed stabilized earth blocks (CSEB).

Community Participatory Co-Design

Fond des Blancs, Haiti / 2024

FDB Civic & Cultural Center Core

Conceived as a multi-scalar civic backbone co-authored directly alongside a Community Planning Advisory Committee (CPAC) to withstand local institutional turbulence. The masterplan integrates local masonry strategies with open internal microclimate courtyards, stepped roofing for air ventilation relief, and self-sufficient rainwater harvesting loops to serve as a community sanctuary.

Abudwak, Somalia / 2024

Iftiin Foundation Peace Hub

A modular humanitarian civic node built in partnership with the Iftiin Foundation and USAID to stabilize communities experiencing severe resource displacement. Uses a circular plan wrapped around an absolute shade courtyard, utilizing thick local limestone walls for thermal cooling mass, passive wind ventilation openings, and rainwater reservoirs to lower reliance on fragile municipal grids.
