01 / ABOUT

The short version

Early in my practice as a registered architect in Nairobi, I realized that even the most thoughtfully designed building stops at its plot line—it cannot single-handedly fix an unsafe transit corridor or an unlit underpass.

That realization reshaped my practice, driving me to city science at the Norman Foster Institute in Madrid to bridge spatial data analytics (QGIS, Python) with human-centered, buildable master planning across multi-modal transit interchanges and fragile urban environments.
