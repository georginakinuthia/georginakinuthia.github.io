Bio

I specialize in using spatial data and participatory design to support more equitable infrastructure planning in resource-constrained cities.

Trained as an architect at the University of Nairobi, I've since practiced across Kenya, Haiti, and Somalia — delivering civic, educational, and humanitarian infrastructure for partners including the FAO, USAID, and SHOFCO. I'm currently a Scholar at the Norman Foster Institute in Madrid, building GIS-based spatial vulnerability frameworks for Mexico City's Miguel Hidalgo borough.

Spatial Vulnerability & Infrastructure Equity

GIS-based diagnostics to identify and address infrastructure and safety gaps in underserved urban areas.

Participatory & Community-Led Design

Embedding local governance structures, including community planning committees, directly into the design process.

Circular & Resilient Infrastructure

Low-carbon, locally sourced construction systems for climate-vulnerable, resource-constrained contexts.
