Bio

I am an Urban Systems Architect and researcher using spatial data, participatory planning, and climate-responsive design to tackle infrastructure inequalities in growing cities [INDEX: 0.1.3]. My work connects hard data—like GIS mapping and Python modeling—with the real-world needs of local communities [INDEX: 0.1.33]. From designing schools in rural Haiti to analyzing transit accessibility in Mexico City, I build evidence-based frameworks that help city planners, architecture studios, and NGOs direct funding where it matters most [INDEX: 0.1.20, 0.1.36].
