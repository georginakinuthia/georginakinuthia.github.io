01 / ABOUT

The short version

Early in my practice as a registered architect in Nairobi, I realized that even the most thoughtfully designed building stops at its plot line—it cannot single-handedly fix an unsafe transit corridor, an unlit underpass, or an unmanaged municipal waste stream.

That realization reshaped my practice, driving me to city science at the Norman Foster Institute in Madrid. I specialize in bridging spatial data science with physical construction reality—translating empirical open datasets and network accessibility models into buildable public realm master plans, low-carbon masonry assemblies, and resilient civic infrastructure.
