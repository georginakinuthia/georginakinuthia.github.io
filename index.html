01 / MANIFESTO

From Drafting to City Science

Early in my practice as a licensed architect in Nairobi, I realized that even the most thoughtfully designed building stops at its plot line, as it cannot single-handedly fix an unsafe transit corridor, an unlit underpass, or an unmanaged municipal waste stream. That realization brought me to Madrid and the Norman Foster Institute to bridge spatial data science with physical master planning.

With over six years of hands-on architectural execution across East Africa, Haiti, and Somalia, I treat spatial data as a decision-support tool to serve human experience. I combine QGIS spatial modeling and Python automated batch pipelines with real-world construction logic, low-carbon materials, and deep community co-design. My focus is simple: turning complex spatial diagnostics into buildable, climate-resilient urban infrastructure that protects dignity and improves daily public life.
