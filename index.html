Bio

I am Georgina Kinuthia, a sustainable urban designer and architect using spatial data and participatory design to close infrastructure equity gaps in resource-constrained cities. Trained as an architect at the University of Nairobi, I have since practiced across Kenya, Haiti, and Somalia — delivering civic, educational, and humanitarian infrastructure for partners including the FAO, USAID, and SHOFCO.

In my current role as a Scholar at the Norman Foster Institute in Madrid, my focus lies in the development of GIS-based spatial vulnerability frameworks to identify risk distribution channels across Mexico City's Miguel Hidalgo borough. By parsing empirical data alongside neighborhood governance networks, I structure multi-scalar action indices that direct public funding into targeted resilient interventions.
