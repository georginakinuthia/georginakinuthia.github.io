01 / VISION & FUTURE FOCUS

From Drafting to City Science

Across six years of professional architectural execution in East Africa, I managed the construction delivery of over 12,000 m² of built space. Yet, physical buildings alone cannot solve systemic urban challenges like unsafe transit corridors or unmanaged waste streams. My transition to computational city science in Europe bridges this gap—combining physical design instincts with data science to make spatial analytics buildable and policy-relevant.

Evidence-Based Public Realm

Leveraging multi-layer spatial diagnostics (safety mapping, transit catchments) to inform equitable municipal policy and transit-oriented development.

Circular & Low-Carbon Infrastructure

Scaling automated spatial siting models for circular urban waste hubs and decentralized green utilities.

Global-South Rigor for Global Cities

Applying resource-efficient, high-vulnerability diagnostic frameworks to European and international urban contexts.

Seeking urban planning, spatial analytics, and infrastructure strategy roles within multidisciplinary delivery teams in Madrid and globally.
