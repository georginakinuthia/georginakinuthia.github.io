Services

My practice bridges spatial data analytics, participatory design, and climate-responsive architecture to tackle infrastructure gaps in resource-constrained urban environments. I design evidence-based solutions that translate complex city data into clear, community-rooted master plans and spatial strategies.

Expertise Framework

1. GIS & Spatial Vulnerability Analytics

QGIS Google Colab
  • Multi-Layer Vulnerability Mapping: I build custom spatial risk frameworks (such as the V.E.R.A. index for Mexico City) that stack multi-layered GIS datasets to identify hidden spatial equity gaps, safety discrepancies, and transit connectivity barriers.
  • Site Suitability & Network Optimization: I utilize geographic spatial overlay models to optimize site selection for public, resilient infrastructure based on local waste flows, accessibility thresholds, and socioeconomic heatmaps.

2. Community Co-Design & Inclusive Spaces

Notion Google Workspace Notebook Verbal Presentations
  • Community Advisory & Workshop Facilitation: I form and guide local Community Planning Advisory Committees (CPACs)—bringing together elders, youth leaders, and women's groups—to ensure technical planning reflects lived realities and resident priorities.
  • Trauma-Informed & Universal Design Protocols: I design secure, supportive environments tailored for vulnerable populations (including GBV survivors and displaced youth), integrating careful privacy gradients, sensory regulation, and full paraplegic accessibility features.
  • Field Research & Field Needs Assessment: I lead localized household surveys and participatory workshops, packaging results into clear verbal presentations and data-tracking matrices to inform technical master plans.

3. Sustainable Master Planning & Climate Design

ArchiCAD Revit Rhino Grasshopper Generative Designs
  • Low-Carbon Material Specification: I integrate locally available, regenerative building systems (such as compressed earth blocks, structural bamboo, and stone) to minimize embodied carbon while strengthening regional economies and labor chains.
  • Passive Climate Engineering: I utilize computational and parametric workflows to design site-responsive massing, stack-effect ventilation chimneys, and deep shading overhangs that lower indoor temperatures by 4–6°C naturally.
  • Off-Grid Utility Systems Integration: I design self-sufficient closed-loop mechanics directly into regional layouts, mapping rainwater harvesting loops, solar-hybrid microgrids, and nature-based stormwater management lines.

4. Circular Economy & Urban Metabolism Research

MS Office
  • Regenerative Infrastructure Feasibility: I conduct spatial feasibility research for decentralized circular facilities. For instance, I contributed to the early-stage conceptualization and research for Kenya's urban resilience strategy under the FAO Green Cities Initiative.
