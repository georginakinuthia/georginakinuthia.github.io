Services

As a sustainable urban designer and architect, I partner with spatial data analysts, design practices, and local municipalities to close spatial infrastructure equity gaps.

My methodology focuses on collaborative workflows, ensuring all visual metrics and dashboard tools developed are easily maintainable by your internal community leaders and team structures.

  • Spatial vulnerability indexing and baseline diagnostics
  • Participatory co-design workshop frameworks
  • Low-carbon, local material circularity masterplanning
  • Pedestrian path analysis and transit corridor metrics
  • Interactive web map dashboards and spatial visualizations

Tools & Software Competencies

I leverage an array of spatial mapping, programming, and visualization engines to support planning pipelines. Common toolkit selections include:

QGIS ArcGIS Pro Python (GeoPandas) PostgreSQL / PostGIS AutoCAD Rhino 3D Adobe Creative Suite
